HOW IT WORKS · SEC 03

Watch. Map. Respond. Mark. Draft. Reverse.

01WATCH

Watches the FDA list

Syncs the FDA drug shortage feed on a daily schedule and matches it to your hospital's drug list — by NDC first, then RxNorm ingredient and dosage form.

02MAP IMPACT

Maps what it touches

Finds the Confluence pages and open Jira work that name the drug, each item's owner and team, and earlier responses to the same drug.

03RESPOND

Runs the response in Jira

Opens one work item per shortage, a task per content owner, and a panel with the FDA facts and the impact map — with consent from your admin settings.

04MARK PAGES

Marks affected pages

Shows a notice on each affected Confluence page — visible in the byline — linking straight to the Jira response.

05DRAFT

Drafts from your own history

A Rovo agent drafts the conservation plan and clinician message from your site's own past decisions. A person approves before anything is written.

06REVERSE

Reverses the workarounds

When the FDA marks the shortage resolved, opens a task for every temporary change and clears the page notices.

BOUNDARIES · SEC 04

What it will not do

  • Holds no patient data

    Never asks for, stores or displays patient information. Inbound data is public FDA and NLM drug data only. Nothing about your site is sent to an outside host.

  • Never recommends a substitute drug

    No clinical advice. It shows only alternatives your own pages already approve, quoted with a link — and says so when none are documented.

  • Shows no unsourced fact

    Every statement links to its FDA record, Confluence page or Jira work item. If a scope or product is unreadable, the app says what it could not read.

INSTALL · SEC 07

Running on your site in minutes

  1. Install Shortfall from the installation link on a site with Rovo enabled.
  2. Grant the Teamwork Graph scopes when prompted — an organization admin approves them.
  3. Open Shortfall in Jira's Apps menu and choose which Confluence spaces hold clinical content.
  4. Add your drug list — paste, upload a CSV, or load the sample hospital.
  5. Run the first sync. Matched shortages, impact maps and owners appear on the dashboard.
